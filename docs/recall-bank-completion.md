# Recall bank completion

The Russia Recall bank now contains 150 questions: 25 in each of the six chronological course sections. The expansion follows the 2026-27 AQA 1H curriculum map and covers political authority, opposition, economy, society, reform/repression, war and major turning points.

Quality rules:
- Existing question IDs remain unchanged so saved student statistics remain valid.
- MCQs use four plausible options and are automatically rotated across A/B/C/D at runtime.
- The build validator rejects expanded MCQs where the correct option is conspicuously longer than the alternatives.
- Short-answer questions include common name/term variants.
- Normalisation ignores punctuation, apostrophes, hyphens and case.
- Typed answers of 5-9 characters allow one edit; answers of 10+ characters allow up to two edits, with adjacent transpositions also accepted.
- Questions prioritise specification-useful AO1 rather than disconnected trivia.
